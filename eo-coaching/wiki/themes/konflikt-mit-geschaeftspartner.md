# Konflikt mit Geschäftspartner / Mitgründer

**Worum es geht:** Spannung mit einem Partner — mangelnde Führung, aufgeschobene Konfrontation, ein Thema, das seit Jahren ungelöst bleibt. In den Quellen ein Standardbeispiel für ein EQ-Thema — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]], [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~17:18).

## Passende Formate

- [[deep-dive]] (geplant), wenn eine Entscheidung reift; [[open-coaching]], wenn es akut in der 5%-Reflection auftaucht. Fast immer EQ → volles Coaching — [[eq-vs-iq-topics]] *(inference)*.

## Gute Fragen

1. „What is a single headline for the topic and the tension?“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
2. „What would your business partner say if they were in the room?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~23:42)
3. „In what ways are you contributing to the issue?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
4. „Are your objectives in conflict with those of other people? Who and how?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
5. „What is holding you back from choosing to do something different than what you're doing now?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~19:53)
6. „What are the benefits of doing nothing?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
7. „What would you like to be able to say, 1 year from now?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]

**Share-Requests:**
- Near-exact: „A time when you felt undermined by a business partner's lack of leadership.“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
- Gleiche Emotion: „A time when you felt that same tension confronting someone on an awkward topic.“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
- Eine Ebene höher: „What's a time when you really cared about someone and you'd been putting off a conversation and you … wanted to confront them in their own best interests …?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~40:19)

## Worauf achten

- Nicht in Partner-Bashing oder Lösungsdebatten abrutschen — der Coach definiert, er löst nicht — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~8:46).
- Wiederkehrendes Thema ohne Handlung: das Forum wird ungeduldig („when are you going to decide …“); die Sprecher haben dafür kein Best Practice — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~16:50, ~20:26). Ansatz: Accountability-Level erfragen (1–10) — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~20:51).
- „Even if you choose not to decide you still have made a choice“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~19:32).

## Wenn ich selbst Presenter bin

- Im Worksheet eine Deutung wählen („A Crossroads“, „Necessary Evil“, „Ongoing Battle“ …) und ehrlich ausfüllen: „I'm complicating things by …“, „I've behaved this way before when …“ — [[forum-playbook-planned-deep-dive-worksheet]].
- Vorab sagen, welche Unterstützung ich will (Check-ins, Accountability, Ressourcen) — [[forum-coaching-podcast-1]] (~48:34).
