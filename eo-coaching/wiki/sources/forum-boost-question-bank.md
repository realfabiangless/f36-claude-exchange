# Forum Boost – Question Bank

**What it is:** Eine einseitige Fragenbank mit 20 Coaching-Fragen aus dem „Forum Boost“-Material von ForumSherpa (©2022, „For use within your Forum only“, Seite 8 eines größeren Dokuments). Im Projekt als „Open Coaching Questions“ abgelegt. Die PDF-Metadaten nennen Muhammad Kawish als Autor und Februar 2024 als Erstellungsdatum.

## Summary

- Reine Fragenliste ohne Ablauf oder Erklärung — Werkzeugkasten für [[open-coaching]] und die Vorbereitung eines [[deep-dive]].
- Die Fragen decken fünf Stoßrichtungen ab *(Gruppierung: inference)*: Vorgeschichte und Muster, Lücke Ist/Soll, Wirkung und Dynamik des Problems, Grenzen und Ressourcen, Risiken und Szenarien.
- Mehrere Fragen öffnen bewusst die Perspektive gegen den Strich: was gut läuft, wer widersprechen würde, welchen Nutzen Nichtstun hätte.

## Key claims

Die 20 Fragen im Wortlaut (nummeriert wie im Original):

1. „Has this issue or something similar occurred before? How and when?“
2. „What is the gap between where you are today and where you want to be in this issue?“
3. „What is the impact of the problem on you? On others?“
4. „Is the issue growing, shrinking or staying the same?“
5. „What causes the issue to grow, shrink or stay the same?“
6. „What is NOT a problem related to this issue? What is going right or working well?“
7. „Is there a time parameter affecting the issue?“
8. „Are there financial limitations affecting the issue?“
9. „What other limitations might be affecting the issue?“
10. „Could you solve part of the problem?“
11. „Are your objectives in conflict with those of other people? Who and how?“
12. „What are the benefits of doing nothing?“
13. „How can the Forum/group help you?“
14. „Are there any other people, tools or resources that could help you with this issue?“
15. „Can you break down the issue into smaller pieces to clarify the situation?“
16. „Who would disagree with you on this issue, what would they disagree with and why?“
17. „What is the biggest obstacle or concern related to this issue? If that object were magically removed, what would you do?“ (z. B. „if money were no object…“)
18. „What is at risk to you, personally, in dealing with this situation? What level of risk is acceptable to you?“
19. „If you could wave a magic wand to fix this, what would the ‚fix' look like?“
20. „What is the worst-case scenario and the best-case scenario you can imagine as an outcome?“
