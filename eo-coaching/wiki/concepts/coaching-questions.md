# Coaching-Fragen

**Was diese Seite ist:** Die gesammelten Fragen aller Quellen, geordnet nach Phase eines Coachings — vom Orientieren über die Fakten zum Kernkonflikt bis zum Share-Request. Für [[open-coaching]] und das Vorgespräch eines [[deep-dive]]. Die Sortierung nach Phase ist *(inference)*; jede Frage trägt ihre Quelle.

## Regeln für gute Fragen

- **Offen:** Was mit Ja, Nein oder einem Wort beantwortbar ist, ist schlecht; kurze Faktenfragen am Anfang sind erlaubt — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~19:37).
- **Nicht suggestiv:** „Have you tried this yet?“ ist tabu — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~20:22).
- **Nicht belehrend:** „Why haven't you done that?“, „Who's your accountant?“ lassen den Presenter Mauern bauen („everybody knows more than me and I look like an idiot“) — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~29:43–30:14).
- Faktenfragen neutral einleiten: „Let me just get a couple facts about the situation“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~31:07).

## 1. Orientieren

1. „What is a single headline for the topic and the tension?“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
2. „What are 3 emotions related to the topic?“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
3. „What types of experiences are you curious to hear from the group?“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]], [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~21:12)
4. „What do you want out of this?“ / „What are you trying to solve?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~7:06)
5. „What do you want from the group?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~20:51)

## 2. Fakten und Dynamik (kurz halten)

6. „Has this issue or something similar occurred before? How and when?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
7. „Is the issue growing, shrinking or staying the same?“ / „What causes the issue to grow, shrink or stay the same?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
8. „What is the gap between where you are today and where you want to be in this issue?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
9. „What is NOT a problem related to this issue? What is going right or working well?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
10. „Is there a time parameter …?“, „Are there financial limitations …?“, „What other limitations …?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
11. „Can you break down the issue into smaller pieces to clarify the situation?“ / „Could you solve part of the problem?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]

## 3. Zum Kern (unter die Wasserlinie — [[iceberg-model]])

12. „…and why is that significant?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
13. „What is the most troubling part of this for you?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
14. „When have you felt like this before?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
15. „In what ways are you contributing to the issue?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
16. „How do you feel about …?“ / „What's your relationship …?“ / „What would it look like if …?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~20:22)
17. „How does that make you feel, saying that in front of someone else?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~22:58)
18. „What is the impact of the problem on you? On others?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
19. „What is at risk to you, personally, in dealing with this situation? What level of risk is acceptable to you?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
20. „What is holding you back from choosing to do something different than what you're doing now?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~19:53)
21. „Which struggle is strongest?“ — [[open-coaching-and-deep-dives-grafik]]
22. Die Gefühle-Ebene ansteuern: Unsicherheiten, Identität, Beziehungen, persönliche Werte, Vertrauen — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]

## 4. Perspektive wechseln

23. „If your friend was sharing this issue, what question would you ask them?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]]
24. „What would the closest loved one to you say about this?“ / „What would your business partner say if they were in the room?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~23:42); hilft, wenn in angespannten Lagen die Empathie wegfällt (~24:12)
25. „Who would disagree with you on this issue, what would they disagree with and why?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
26. „Are your objectives in conflict with those of other people? Who and how?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
27. „What are the benefits of doing nothing?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]

## 5. Zukunft und Möglichkeiten

28. „What would you like to be able to say, 1 year from now?“ — [[forum-playbook-deep-dive-coachs-worksheet]], [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~23:42)
29. „If you could wave a magic wand to fix this, what would the ‚fix' look like?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
30. „What is the biggest obstacle …? If that obstacle were magically removed, what would you do?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
31. „What is the worst-case scenario and the best-case scenario …?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]
32. „What outcome would you like to see?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~25:29)

## 6. Unterstützung klären

33. „How can the Forum support you?“ / „Are you asking for check-ins, are you asking for accountability, do you need resources, what is it?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-1]] (~48:28)
34. „On a scale of one to ten what level of accountability do you want?“ — [[forum-coaching-podcast-3]] (~20:51)
35. „Are there any other people, tools or resources that could help you with this issue?“ — [[forum-boost-question-bank]]

## 7. Abschluss: Share-Request

36. „A time when you felt…“ — in drei Varianten (near-exact, gleiche Emotion/andere Situation, die andere Seite); Details in [[experience-sharing]] — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
37. Für IQ-Themen: „How did you…“ / „What did you do when…“ — [[forum-playbook-open-coaching]]
38. Erfolgstest: die Experience-Frage am Ende wiederholen — [[forum-coaching-podcast-2]] (~21:37)
